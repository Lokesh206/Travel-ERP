Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to understand the referral journey.

Who can join the Referral Program?

TravelEasy customers and eligible registered users can participate according to the applicable program rules.

How do I make a referral?

After joining, use your referral facility to share TravelEasy with people who may be interested in booking a journey.

When is a referral considered successful?

A referral becomes eligible when the applicable requirements for a qualifying booking are completed.

Can my referral level change?

Yes. Eligible activity can contribute to progression through the TravelEasy referral levels.
