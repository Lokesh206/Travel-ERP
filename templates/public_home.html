THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Booking & Travel Policies.
Clear terms, total transparency.

Review and check off our transparent travel standards before browsing journeys. All terms are guaranteed.

4 of 4 policies accepted ✓
Booking & payment Verified Policy

Bookings can be requested directly online with zero hidden surcharges. Seats, travel dates, and accommodation plans are confirmed promptly by our travel desk before any payment is finalized.

  • 💳 Transparent Schedule: Simple 25% deposit on booking confirmation; balance payable 10 days prior to departure.
  • 🔒 Price Lock Guarantee: Quoted price in INR is 100% all-inclusive (stays, daily breakfast, private AC transfers & monument entries).
Cancellation & refund policy Clear Slabs

We provide transparent, tiered refund protection with no hidden cancellation penalties.

  • 📅 More than 7 days prior: 90% full refund directly returned to your source account.
  • ⏳ 3 to 7 days prior: 50% refund, or 100% travel credit voucher valid for 12 months.
  • ⚡ Within 72 hours: Subject strictly to hotel and transit provider non-refundable policies.
Privacy & your information Strict Privacy

Your booking details, contact numbers, and travel requests are securely handled solely for coordinating your itinerary and travel support.

  • 🛡️ No Marketing Spam: We never sell, share, or disclose your personal details to third-party telemarketers.
  • 📲 Concierge Channel: Updates delivered exclusively through official WhatsApp & SMS for travel vouchers and driver dispatch.
Terms & regulations Shrine Protocol

All shrine darshans, temple visits, and monument tours strictly follow local devasthanams protocol, traditional dress codes, and Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) guidelines.

  • 🏛️ Official Portal Transparency: We link official government/trust portals directly on each package for live darshan & ticket notices.
  • 👔 Devasthanam Dress Codes: Traditional modest attire (dhoti/kurta/sari) advised for holy sanctums; guided assistance provided.
